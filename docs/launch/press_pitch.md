# Press pitch — consumer angle (~150 words)

**Subject:** New benchmark: AI transcribes your words perfectly but scores 46% at hearing how you feel

Hi [name],

Millions of people now talk to AI daily — and companies are wiring these models into call centers, cars, and therapy-adjacent apps that claim to sense frustration or distress. We measured whether they actually can.

Oruk AI's new speech-emotion benchmark tested every major model on 64,384 voice clips across ~20 languages: does the speaker sound angry, happy, sad, afraid, disgusted, surprised, or neutral? A task humans do without thinking.

The three most quotable results:

- **The best frontier AI model scored 46.0%.** Random guessing scores 14.3%.
- **A model given only the transcript — no audio at all — scored 39–40%.** The "listening" models are mostly reading the words.
- **Frontier models nearly can't hear disgust:** 0.13–0.19 F1, versus 0.905 for a small specialized model.

Full leaderboard, open methodology, and researchers available for interview. Interested?

Nathan Roll, Oruk AI
